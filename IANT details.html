The Institute

IANT (Institute of Advance Network Technology) is a premier institution dedicated to advancing the field of network technology through cutting-edge research, education, and innovation. Established with the vision of fostering excellence in networking, IANT serves as a hub for students, researchers, and industry professionals to collaborate and push the boundaries of what is possible in the realm of computer networks.
